FAQ SECTION

Add these questions:

1. Can I book online?
   Answer:
   Not yet. For now, all bookings start with a WhatsApp or contact request so we can check your arrival details and make sure the right pack fits your situation.

2. Are prices fixed?
   Answer:
   Starter and Ready prices are fixed launch prices. Melbourne Arrival is a limited launch offer available for selected arrival dates and selected hostel options.

3. Is accommodation included in Melbourne Arrival?
   Answer:
   Yes, Melbourne Arrival includes 7 nights in a selected shared hostel dorm. 4-bed dorm and private room upgrades may be available on request.

4. Do you guarantee a job?
   Answer:
   We help with preparation, CV, job search direction and practical guidance. Work depends on applications, availability and employer decisions.

5. Do you provide migration advice?
   Answer:
   No. OZ Connection does not provide migration advice. For visa-related matters, we can connect clients with a registered migration agent partner when required.

6. Can parents contact OZ Connection?
   Answer:
   Yes, with the Parents Peace of Mind add-on. Updates are shared only with the traveller’s consent.

7. Can I add Parents Peace of Mind later?
   Answer:
   Yes. Parents Peace of Mind can be added anytime before or after arrival.

8. Is Mail Holding included?
   Answer:
   Mail Holding is available as a 12-month add-on for Australia Starter and Melbourne Ready. It is included in Melbourne Arrival.

9. What if my arrival date does not work for Melbourne Arrival?
   Answer:
   We will let you know and suggest the best available option. Melbourne Ready may still be available to help you prepare your first two weeks properly.

10. Is OZ Connection available outside Melbourne?
    Answer:
    Australia Starter can support clients arriving anywhere in Australia. Melbourne Ready and Melbourne Arrival are focused on Melbourne.
